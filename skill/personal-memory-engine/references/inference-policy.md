# Inference policy

Prefer evidence in this order: explicit correction or durable preference; user self-explanation, concept distinction, product judgment, or reviewed artifact; repeated behavior across independent contexts; a single behavior or phrasing pattern.

Do not use a question by itself as evidence of ignorance, inability, personality, or a stable preference. Do not infer diagnoses, protected traits, intimate attributes, or broad personality labels. Do not convert temporary emotion or task-specific wording into a durable claim.

Create every new hypothesis as `candidate`. Propose `active_hypothesis` only with at least two independent supporting events, no unresolved strong counterevidence, and a narrow scope. Propose `confirmed_principle` only after explicit confirmation or sustained high-consistency evidence. Add counterevidence instead of deleting inconvenient evidence. Never perform a state upgrade while `runtime/mode.json` says `shadow`.

Store the shortest quote that still proves the observation. Separate quote, observation, and inference. If a fact would not change future collaboration, do not save it.
